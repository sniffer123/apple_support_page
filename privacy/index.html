LightAssist 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 3 日 · 版本 1.0

LightAssist 是一款本机优先的日程记录工具。开发者不运营用于保存日程的服务器，不投放广告，也不使用第三方分析或跨 App 跟踪。

本机数据

日程、草稿、设置和所选图片保存在你的设备中。API Key 使用系统安全存储保存，不写入普通数据库或日志。设备备份是否包含这些数据由你的 Apple 或设备备份设置决定。

第三方 AI 服务

只有在你明确选择“AI 整理”、提交语音记录或重新整理事项，并同意发送数据后，LightAssist 才会把当前文字和你选择的图片发送给你配置的第三方 AI 服务。API Key 会随请求直接发送给该服务用于认证。服务商可能按其隐私政策将请求与你的服务商账户关联并保留数据；请在使用前查看相应服务商的政策。开发者不会收到这些请求内容。

语音、照片与剪贴板

iPhone 语音识别被要求在设备端执行；原始录音不会由 LightAssist 保存或发送给日程解析模型，只有转写文字可能在你同意后发送。照片只通过系统选择器读取你主动选择的项目。剪贴板只在你点击粘贴操作时读取。

通知与权限

麦克风、语音识别、照片和通知权限只用于对应功能。拒绝权限不会阻止你使用手工新增和查看日程等不依赖该权限的功能。

保留、删除与撤回

删除事项时，LightAssist 会删除对应数据库记录和不再被其他事项或草稿引用的附件文件。你可以在“设置 → 数据与隐私”撤回第三方 AI 数据发送同意，或清除全部事项、草稿、附件、AI 配置和 API Key。卸载 App 会移除设备上的 App 数据，但已有设备或云备份由相应备份服务管理。

安全与责任

LightAssist 使用 HTTPS 连接你选择的 AI 服务，并在系统安全存储中保存 API Key。你负责选择可信的服务商、保护 API Key，并确认服务商条款允许你的使用方式。

联系我们

如有隐私或数据删除问题，请联系开发者：hzk47st@outlook.com。

LightAssist Privacy Policy

Effective November 3, 2026 · Version 1.0

LightAssist is a local-first schedule capture tool. The developer does not operate a server that stores your schedules, does not serve ads, and does not use third-party analytics or cross-app tracking.

On-device data

Schedules, drafts, settings, and selected images are stored on your device. API keys are stored in secure system storage and are not written to the regular database or logs. Whether device backups include this data is controlled by your Apple or device backup settings.

Third-party AI services

Only after you choose an AI organizing action and consent to sending data does LightAssist send the current text and selected images to the third-party AI service you configured. Your API key is sent directly to that service for authentication. The provider may associate requests with your provider account and retain data under its own privacy policy. Review that policy before use. The developer does not receive the request content.

Voice, photos, and clipboard

On iPhone, speech recognition is required to run on device. LightAssist does not save raw audio or send it to the schedule parsing model; only transcribed text may be sent after you consent. Photos are read through the system picker only when you select them. The clipboard is read only after you invoke a paste action.

Notifications and permissions

Microphone, speech recognition, photo, and notification permissions are used only for their corresponding features. Denying a permission does not prevent use of manual schedule features that do not depend on it.

Retention, deletion, and withdrawal

Deleting a schedule removes its database record and attachment files that are no longer referenced by another schedule or draft. In Settings → Data & Privacy, you can withdraw consent for third-party AI transfers or clear all schedules, drafts, attachments, AI configuration, and API keys. Uninstalling the app removes its on-device app data; existing device or cloud backups are managed by the applicable backup service.

Security and responsibility

LightAssist uses HTTPS to connect to your selected AI service and stores API keys in secure system storage. You are responsible for selecting a trusted provider, protecting the API key, and confirming that the provider terms permit your use.

Contact

For privacy or deletion questions, contact the developer at hzk47st@outlook.com.
